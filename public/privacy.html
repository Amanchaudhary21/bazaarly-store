Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

ShopNest is a practice online store made for learning. It is a demo: there are no real products, payments or deliveries.

What ShopNest stores

When you use ShopNest, this information is saved only on your own device, in the app's browser storage:

  • your account: name, email address or phone number, and a password if you set one
  • your cart, wish list, orders, reviews and recently viewed items
  • photos and details of items you add with "Sell an item"
  • your light or dark theme choice

What ShopNest sends

ShopNest has no server and no database. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking.

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. If "Sign in with Google" is turned on and you choose it, Google handles that sign-in under Google's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your information

Use Reset demo data at the bottom of the store, sign out, or uninstall the app. Clearing the app's or browser's site data also removes everything.

Children

ShopNest does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because nothing leaves your device.
